Remembering Room

Half an hour of the songs, films and shows from their younger years.

Tell us a little about someone you visit. We'll put together a calm session from the years they remember best, with gentle ways to start a conversation.

Nothing you type is saved. It is used once to make the session, then forgotten.

    How did it go?

    After your visit, tell us in a sentence or two. We'll swap what didn't land for something closer to what did.

    How this session was made

      Recommendations come from Qloo's taste data. A language model reads your description and writes the conversation starters. Every item is checked against the person's memory years.

      Remembering Room is a conversation aid, not a medical or care service. Go gently, and follow their lead.
